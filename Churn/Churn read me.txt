##-1-Why is it important to standardize categorical values (e.g., lowercase) before encoding?

##-2-How could we address class imbalance when training models later on?
####-  We only have 2 clases Churners and non churners, so we can use resample (oversampling the minority class, SMOTE or undersampling majority class) or adjusting weights by declaring missing a churner is worse than misclassifying and by using evaluation metrics like precision, recall, and F1 score.
